About FlightPlanPro

FlightPlanPro offers free flight planning calculators and guides for student pilots, pilots and dispatchers.

Disclaimer

Results are estimates for training and planning reference. They are not certified dispatch or flight-planning software and do not replace the aircraft flight manual, approved performance data, official weather, NOTAMs or regulations. The pilot in command and operator remain responsible for all decisions.

Privacy

Calculators run entirely in your browser; the values you enter are not sent anywhere.

Not for operational use. FlightPlanPro tools are for training and planning reference only. Always verify against your aircraft's approved manual (AFM/POH), current weather and NOTAMs, and applicable regulations.
