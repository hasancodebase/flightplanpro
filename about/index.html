About FlightPlanPro

Free aviation calculators and practical learning resources for pilots, student pilots, flight dispatchers, and on-the-job trainers around the world.

Who creates FlightPlanPro

FlightPlanPro is developed by Naeem ul Hasan, a retired Flight Operations Officer based in Pakistan. He has more than 20 years of experience in airline flight operations and flight dispatch, including dispatch work involving EDTO and transatlantic flights.

Naeem holds an MSc in Mathematics and works with AI automation and software development. FlightPlanPro is one of his public projects: a collection of free tools and learning resources for people studying or working in aviation operations.

What the site offers

FlightPlanPro includes browser-based calculators, aviation reference tools, and plain-English guides. The calculators are implemented in code and use mathematical methods for training and planning reference. Their assumptions and limits matter: results can be simplified, incomplete, or wrong.

FlightPlanPro is not certified or approved for operational flight planning, dispatch release, navigation, or load control. Always verify decisions with the current aircraft flight manual, approved operator procedures, official weather and NOTAM sources, and applicable regulations. Read the aviation safety disclaimer and data sources.
