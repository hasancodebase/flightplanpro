How the maths works

  • Moment = weight × arm. CG arm = total moment ÷ total weight.
  • %MAC = (CG arm − LEMAC) ÷ MAC length × 100.
  • ZFW = everything except fuel. TOW = ZFW + ramp fuel − taxi fuel. Landing weight = TOW − trip fuel.
  • Fuel is held at one arm here. On a real aircraft the fuel arm changes with the quantity in each tank, so use the fuel index table from your manual.
  • The trim setting is read from your table by linear interpolation. Real stabilizer trim comes from the flight crew operating manual.
