Flight Fuel Planning Calculator

Estimate block fuel from distance, ground speed and fuel burn. Use the same fuel unit (kg, lb or litres) for burn rate, alternate and taxi fuel.

Formula

Block fuel = taxi + trip + contingency + alternate + final reserve (+ any extra). Trip fuel = (distance ÷ ground speed) × burn rate. Contingency and reserve minima depend on your regulator and operation.

FAQ

What is included in block fuel?

Taxi fuel, trip fuel, contingency fuel, alternate fuel, final reserve fuel and any extra fuel the commander decides to carry.
