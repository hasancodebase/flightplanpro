EDTO Planner: Diversion Circles and Route Coverage

Draw the diversion circles around your en-route alternates and check whether your route stays within the diversion time you plan to use. Learn the basics in the EDTO guide.

Training tool. It measures distance only. It does not check airport status, weather, rescue and firefighting category or your approval. Real EDTO planning uses your aircraft's approved one-engine-inoperative speed and your State's rules.

How it works

  • Diversion distance = (one-engine-inoperative speed + wind component) × diversion time. With no wind this is still-air distance, the basis ICAO uses for the approval.
  • The route is sampled every 5 NM along the great circle. At each point the tool finds the nearest alternate.
  • Points farther than the threshold distance from every alternate are the EDTO portion of the route. Points beyond the diversion distance are not covered, and show in red.

Colours: grey means within the threshold distance of an alternate, green means EDTO but covered, red means not covered by your diversion time.
