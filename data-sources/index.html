Data sources and disclaimer

FlightPlanPro is for training, education and flight simulation. It is not operational flight planning software.

Sources

  • METAR and TAF: NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov), fetched through our server and cached for about five minutes.
  • Airports: OurAirports (ourairports.com), public domain, updated periodically.
  • Aircraft values: rounded training approximations. They are not from any aircraft flight manual.

Not included

NOTAMs, airways, FIR data and winds aloft are not mirrored here. For NOTAMs always use the official source for the country concerned.

Disclaimer

Results are estimates and may be wrong. Do not use them for real flights, dispatch, load control or any safety decision. Always use current official weather, NOTAMs, approved aircraft data and applicable regulations. FlightPlanPro is not affiliated with ICAO, the FAA, EASA, NOAA or any aircraft manufacturer, and gives no warranty of any kind.

Not for operational use. FlightPlanPro tools are for training and planning reference only. Always verify against your aircraft's approved manual (AFM/POH), current weather and NOTAMs, and applicable regulations.
