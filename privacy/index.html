Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026.

Information processed by the tools

Most calculations run in your browser. Values you enter for route, fuel, weight, performance and other calculations are processed on your device. When you request METAR or TAF weather, the selected airport ICAO identifiers and report type are sent to our Cloudflare-hosted weather endpoint, which requests public reports from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov). Weather responses may be cached briefly. Cloudflare may process technical request data, such as IP address and browser details, for security and site operation.

Advertising and cookies

FlightPlanPro does not currently serve Google AdSense ads. If advertising is introduced, Google and participating third-party vendors may use cookies to serve and measure ads based on prior visits to this site or other sites. You can manage Google ad personalization at Google Ads Settings or opt out of some vendors through aboutads.info. Before ads are served, this policy and the site's consent controls will be updated for the vendors and regions involved. Google requires a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF for personalized ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland.

Search performance

We use Google Search Console to review aggregate search performance such as impressions, clicks and queries. The current site code does not load Google Analytics or AdSense scripts.
