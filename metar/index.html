How to read a METAR

A METAR is an observation of current airport weather. The groups run in a fixed order: station, day and time in UTC, wind, visibility, weather, cloud, temperature and dew point, then pressure. The decoder above also gives the flight category from the lowest broken or overcast layer and the visibility. For the full guide and an example, see the METAR decoder.
