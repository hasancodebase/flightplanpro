Aviation safety disclaimer

FlightPlanPro is for education, training and planning reference. It is not operational flight-planning, dispatch, navigation or load-control software.

Do not use for operational decisions

Do not use FlightPlanPro's calculators, maps, weather decoders or guides as the sole or controlling source for an actual flight, dispatch release, load sheet, fuel decision, route clearance or other safety-critical decision. The tools are not certified, approved or validated for operational use.

Verify with approved and current sources

Always use the current aircraft flight manual (AFM/POH), approved performance data, operator manuals and procedures, applicable regulations and official aeronautical publications. Obtain weather and NOTAMs from the official sources for the relevant States and operation. When any result here differs from an approved or official source, follow the approved or official source.

Estimates and limitations

Calculations and database values may be simplified, incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Weather reports can change, third-party data can be unavailable, and technical errors may occur. Check units, assumptions, inputs and results independently. Availability of this website is not guaranteed.

Responsibility

Flight crews, dispatchers, operators and other users remain responsible for verifying information and making decisions under their applicable approvals, training, procedures and law. FlightPlanPro does not replace professional judgment or an operator's safety-management processes.
